Risk classes

Risk decides what a skip costs.

Low risk

A skipped item is forgiven. The gate stays open.

OPEN

Medium risk

A skipped item is tolerated until someone affirms it.

PARTIAL

High risk

A skipped item is not permitted. It blocks the gate.

BLOCKED

--strict applies the high-risk rule at any risk class. An item left unanswered blocks its gates at every class.
